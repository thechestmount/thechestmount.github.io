Submit an Illinois case

Know about a real Illinois police case that deserves the full bodycam? Send it to us. We only take cases from Illinois right now, and we're upfront about how this works: we pick what goes on the channel, and not every request leads to footage.

What we cover

We focus on real, serious Illinois cases with a story worth telling, such as:

  • Drug investigations, search warrants and narcotics arrests
  • Homicide and violent-crime investigations
  • Threats, standoffs and barricade situations
  • Pursuits, felony stops and other high-tension police encounters
  • Calls that show how police procedure, rights and de-escalation actually play out

The incident must have happened in Illinois and been handled by an Illinois agency (city police, county sheriff or Illinois State Police). Cases from other states won't be reviewed for now.

How this really works

  • We choose what we cover. We read every submission, but we decide which cases go on the channel based on what we think fits. We have the right to decline any submission, and we may not reply to every one.
  • We file the records request. For cases we pick, we send a Freedom of Information Act (FOIA) request to the agency for the bodycam video.
  • Not every request is fulfilled. An agency can deny or delay a request, for example when a case is still open, the footage was never recorded or kept, or it can't be released. Sometimes the footage simply can't be found. Records can also take weeks or months.
  • No promises, no payment. Submitting a case doesn't guarantee a video, and we don't pay for or sell submissions.
  1. You submit

    Send the Illinois case and a link to a news article, press release or public record.

  2. We review it

    We check that it's real, public, from Illinois and a fit for the channel.

  3. We send a FOIA request

    If we pick it, we request the bodycam from the agency. This can take weeks or months.

  4. We publish if it comes through

    If the footage is released and usable, we edit it for context and post it. If you opted in, you're credited as "Case suggested by" on the video page and the credits wall.

* required

What makes a strong submission

  • An Illinois incident with a published news article or official press release
  • The agency name and an approximate date, so our FOIA request goes to the right place
  • A serious case with a clear story: drugs, homicide, threats, standoffs or pursuits
  • Usually a few weeks old or more, since open cases are often withheld

Why a case might be declined

  • It happened outside Illinois
  • It involves a minor
  • It isn't a fit for the channel
  • The agency denied the FOIA request, the case is still open, or no footage exists
  • There's no public source we can verify

Our standards

Everyone shown is presumed innocent until proven guilty in a court of law. The Chest Mount is not affiliated with any police department. We only post what the public records show, we don't publish private information, and we don't take requests to target a person.
